\begin{solution}{exo:g9:project-grid-game:8}

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:g9:project-grid-game:11}
Take a winning path. If it visits some cell twice, cut out everything between
the two visits: the player is in the same place afterwards, so the rest of the
path still works, and it is shorter. Repeat until no cell is visited twice.
The path then visits each floor cell at most once; with $f$ floor cells, it
visits at most $f$ cells, so it makes at most $f - 1$ moves. (With gems and a
gate, the cut may skip a gem, and the argument no longer works.)
\end{solution}
