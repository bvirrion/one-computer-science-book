## Overall judgement

**Counts:** 51 defects — 1 major, 23 minor, 27 nits.

**The book as a whole.** It is sound. A 15–18-year-old can learn from it. The CS is right almost everywhere, the code runs as printed, and the town data hold together across chapters. Calibration holds in every chapter: 15 exercises split 5/6/4, a weekend problem of 18–22 questions, and none in the three project chapters. I found no forbidden content:
- no curriculum or school name in print;
- nothing personal (the account prints as `ada`);
- the security chapters (g10-08, g12-12) stay at the level of principles and defences, with no working exploit.

What the gates miss is mostly **prose and solutions drifting from the code**. The patterns:

1. **Solutions out of sync with re-captured outputs** (R2-040, the only major). The `MEASURED.txt` timing programs were re-run after the solutions were written, and the solutions still quote the old numbers. The other measured chapters (g11-06, g11-09, g12-03, g12-08) were checked and agree.
2. **Listing ranges that cut the file's last lines** (R2-027, R2-036, R2-037, R2-038). Each time, the printed program lacks the `print` or `return` that produces the output shown under it. A sweep of every `\omcode` range against its file found no others.
3. **Outputs of programs never shown** (R2-034), and a caption that quotes a number the output does not show (R2-049).
4. **Claims about what code does that the code does not do**:
   - R2-032: a "comparison" that never compares outputs;
   - R2-047: a test that asserts a hand-typed route rather than the algorithm's answer;
   - R2-041: "what does `show` print" when `show` prints nothing;
   - R2-004 and R2-005: wrong line references.
5. **Wrong explanations behind right answers** (R2-021, R2-026, R2-044, R2-013, R2-010).
6. **Typography in the shared style:**
   - `\code` keeps `\nonfrenchspacing` (R2-046, 84 places);
   - stranded weekend-problem part headings (R2-051);
   - capture directives that push printed listings to line 2 (R2-050).
   Each of these is one fix in `styles/` or `tools/` that covers every book.
7. **Self-references by grade number** inside the grade-10–12 volume (R2-003), and a few terms used before they are defined or taught (R2-002, R2-029, R2-048).

**Book 1's trap classes, swept as the coordinator asked:**
- TikZ scaled units: the two scaled grids (g10-05, g10-06) carry `step=1` in xy units and render on their squares. No tiny circles.
- Range cuts: the four already reported, no new ones.
- Tooling headers: R2-050.
- Exercises copying worked examples: an 8-gram and 6-gram overlap sweep of every exercise against its chapter body found nothing over 30 %.
- Captions claiming what the output does not show: a sweep of every number quoted in an `\omoutput` caption found only R2-049. The rest are derived values, checked by hand.

**By grade.**
- **Grade 10** (11 chapters): solid basics. The weakest spots:
  - g10-06 images and sound: the "photograph" that is an AI image (R2-012), and an overreaching compression conclusion (R2-013);
  - g10-11 web project: the cut `dispatch` (R2-037) and a wrong count of addresses (R2-020);
  - g10-01 and g10-02 carry most of the wording nits.
- **Grade 11** (10 chapters): the strongest maths and proof content (g11-05, g11-06, g11-07 are good). The weak spots:
  - g11-01 gates: the solution of Ex 6 explains the bug wrongly (R2-021); Q19 is overstated (R2-022);
  - g11-10 toy-machine project: the comparison that does not compare (R2-032), a cut listing (R2-038), a figure label clash (R2-033).
