The library opens at nine. The reading room is on the first floor, and the
children's room is on the ground floor, next to the desk where books are
returned. A book that is returned late pays a small fine. The fine is the
same for every book, and the money buys new books for the children's room.
On Saturday the library opens at ten and closes at noon. The desk is closed
on Sunday, but books can be returned through the slot in the door.
