binary resolution alone:
  (1) P(x1) | P(x2)
  (2) ~P(x1) | ~P(x2)
  (3) P(x1) | ~P(x2)   from (1) and (2)

with factoring:
  (1) P(x1) | P(x2)
  (2) ~P(x1) | ~P(x2)
  (3) P(x1)   factor of (1)
  (4) ~P(x1)   factor of (2)
  (5) P(x1) | ~P(x2)   from (1) and (2)
  (6) the empty clause   from (3) and (4)
  6 clauses: refuted
